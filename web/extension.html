This browser can't run the UStudy extension. Open this page in Chrome, Edge, Opera or Brave.

Installing takes a minute by hand, because browsers only install extensions on their own from their store. See the installation guide

Then open it

Click the UStudy icon in your toolbar. It shows your companion, your coins and growth, and the next things due. It uses this UStudy automatically, as long as you stay signed in here.

The extension only looks: it never changes your assignments or spends your coins.
