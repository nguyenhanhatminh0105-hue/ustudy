Install it in three steps

Browsers only install extensions on their own from their store, so this one takes a minute by hand.

  1. 1

    Unzip the download

    Open your Downloads folder, right-click ustudy-extension.zip and choose Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere it won’t be deleted: the browser runs the extension from it.

  2. 2

    Open your extensions page

    Paste this into the address bar, then turn on Developer mode (top right).

    chrome://extensions
  3. 3

    Load it

    Click Load unpacked and choose the folder you unzipped. Pin the UStudy icon from the puzzle-piece menu so it stays in your toolbar.

Then open it

Click the UStudy icon in your toolbar. It shows your companion, your coins and growth, and the next things due. It uses this UStudy automatically, as long as you stay signed in here.

The extension only looks: it never changes your assignments or spends your coins.
